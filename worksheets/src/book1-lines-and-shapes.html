Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Straight Down

Straight Down

Start at the dot. Pull your pencil straight DOWN to the line. “Top... to... bottom!”

A straight line can be a candle, a tree trunk, or a rocket!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Straight Across

Straight Across

Start at the dot. Slide your pencil straight ACROSS to the arrow. “Zoooom, all the way across!”

A straight line across can be a road, a shelf, or a big smile.

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Round and Round

Round and Round

Start at the dot. Go round and round, just like a wheel. “Round... and round... and round!”

Circles make wheels, buttons, and a big bright sun!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Criss Cross

Criss Cross

First pull straight DOWN. Then slide straight ACROSS. Two lines make a cross! “Down... then across!”

Crosses make a plus sign, a kiss, and a bandage on a teddy bear.

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Slanty Line

Slanty Line

Start at the top dot. Slide down to the bottom, like a playground slide. “Slide down the slide!”

A slanty line is a slide, a rocket ramp, or a shooting star trail.

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Slanty Line the Other Way

Slanty Line the Other Way

Start at the top dot. Slide down the OTHER way this time. “Whoosh, the other way!”

Two slanty lines together will soon make a letter A!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Boxy Square

Boxy Square

Trace all four sides: across the top, down the side, across the bottom, up the side. “Across, down, across, up!”

Squares make presents, windows, and picture frames!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Pointy Triangle

Pointy Triangle

Slide up to the point, slide down the other side, then straight across to close it. “Up to the point, then down!”

Triangles make ice-cream cones, party hats, and tall trees.

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Zig Zag

Zig Zag

Bounce your pencil up and down like little mountains. “Zig! Zag! Zig! Zag!”

Zig-zags make mountains, lightning bolts, and crocodile teeth!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Wavy Line

Wavy Line

Curve gently up and down, smooth like water. No sharp corners! “Up and over, like the sea!”

Wavy lines make the sea, a snake, and a wriggly worm.

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Loop the Loop

Loop the Loop

Swing your pencil up, round in a loop, then straight into the next loop. “Round, up, and over!”

Loops are the secret move behind lots of cursive letters — great practice!

Book 1 · Lines & Shapes · Snail Spiral

Snail Spiral

Start in the middle. Curl round and round, getting bigger every time. “Round and round, bigger and bigger!”

Spirals make a snail shell, a lollipop swirl, and curly hair!
