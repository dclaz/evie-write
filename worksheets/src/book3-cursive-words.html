A Note for Grown-ups

☁️

This book uses the VIC Modern Cursive style taught in Victorian schools — the same joined-up handwriting Evie will use for years to come.

Cursive is usually introduced a little later than the toddler years, so there's no rush at all. Let Evie lead: some days might just be finger-tracing the shapes in the air, and that's perfect too.

Encourage one smooth, unlifted stroke where you can — the loops and joins are what make cursive fast and fun once they click!
